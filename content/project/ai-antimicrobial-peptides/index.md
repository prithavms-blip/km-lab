title: "AI-Assisted Antimicrobial Peptide Generation"
summary: "The rapid rise of antibiotic-resistant bacteria has created an urgent need to discover new antimicrobial agents that can effectively fight bacterial infections. This project generated 120,000 peptide sequences and selected 13 promising antimicrobial peptide candidates through AI-based screening and physicochemical filtering. The selected peptides were synthesized with high purity and tested for antibacterial activity. Among them, three peptides showed the most promising activity, with MIC values as low as 2 µg/mL against  S. aureus and 8–16 µg/mL against E. coli. These peptides were further evaluated for mammalian cell compatibility. Ongoing work includes biofilm testing, MBC testing, evaluation against additional drug-resistant bacterial strains, and further optimization of peptide candidates."
