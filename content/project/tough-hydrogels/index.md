title: "Tough Hydrogels for Versatile Applications"
summary: "We are developing tough, self-healing hydrogels that combine a permanent gelatin-based network with a reversible, dynamic polymer networks. By mechanical manipulation of the gels, we lock in molecular entanglements that dramatically increase its strength and stretchability—turning a soft gel into a load-bearing material. Using a combination of advanced rheology, X-ray and neutron scattering, and molecular simulation, we are uncovering how these networks reorganize to program mechanical properties. This work aims to establish design rules for the next generation of injectable, load-bearing hydrogels for applications such as cartilage repair."
