Pritha is a postdoctoral scholar in the MSE Department at UCF. She received her Ph.D. in
Materials Science and Engineering from UCF and completed her undergraduate degree in
Metallurgy and Materials Engineering at the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and
Technology. She is currently developing polymeric systems for hemostasis and wound
healing. Outside the lab, her hobbies include linguistics, art, and cooking.
